PanelOS

Windows tray app for 3.5-inch USB PC case screens (Hardware Id VID_1A86&PID_5722). Shows CPU/GPU temps, load and watts, RAM, VRAM and fan speed, plus the song playing with the real album cover. Six themes switch from the tray. Free Lite installer on GitHub; Pro unlocks all themes and now-playing for a one-time payment.
