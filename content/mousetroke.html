Mousetroke

Free, open-source mouse gestures for Windows. Hold right-click and draw — go back, close tabs, minimize windows — while a plain right-click still opens the context menu. Hot corners, 50+ built-in gestures, ~19 MB RAM. Rust + Tauri. MIT license. Windows 10/11 64-bit. Free, no ads, no account required.
